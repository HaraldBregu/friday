Agentic systems become useful when they can act. They become durable when those
actions are understandable, reversible, and bounded.

The roadmap is organized around three practical safeguards.

## Approval gates

Not every task needs a prompt, but sensitive operations should pause at the
right point. The workflow model should make those points explicit.

## Scoped access

Credentials should be limited to the task. Read-only workflows, temporary
tokens, and clear integration boundaries make mistakes easier to contain.

## Audit trails

Every meaningful change should leave a record: what was requested, what was
inspected, what changed, and where the result was posted.
